Customer financial data has a disposal deadline

Wealth advisors, CPA and tax-prep firms, mortgage brokers, credit unions, and fintech startups all hold data that regulators treat as sensitive by default. When laptops, file servers, and scanners retire, the data on them needs to be destroyed to a standard — not deleted.

EndByte sanitizes every drive to NIST 800-88 or physically destroys it, verifies the result, and issues a certificate per drive. That gives your written information security program a concrete, auditable disposal step.

GLBA Safeguards Rule · 16 CFR 314.4(c)(6)

Financial institutions must securely dispose of customer information no later than two years after it was last used to serve the customer, unless it's still needed or must be retained.

FACTA Disposal Rule · 16 CFR Part 682

Anyone disposing of consumer report information must take reasonable measures to protect it — including destroying or erasing electronic media so the information can't be read or reconstructed.

California · Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.81

California businesses must dispose of customer records containing personal information by shredding, erasing, or otherwise making them unreadable.

General information, not legal advice. Your compliance obligations depend on your organization — check with your counsel or compliance lead.
