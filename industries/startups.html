Data destruction for startups & tech

Offboarded laptops, dead dev machines, and the closet of old hardware from your last office move — handled properly, with the evidence your SOC 2 auditor will ask for.

Your auditor will ask where the old laptops went

Startups churn through hardware: employees leave, laptops die, offices move, and test servers get retired. Every one of those drives holds source code, credentials, and customer data. “We wiped it” isn't evidence — a certificate is.

EndByte sanitizes every drive to NIST 800-88 (or destroys it), verifies it, and issues a certificate per drive with the serial number, so you can match it to your MDM or asset inventory and drop it straight into your audit evidence folder.

Remote team? Individual drives can be mailed in. Office clean-out? We'll pick it all up.

SOC 2 · CC6.5

Organizations discontinue protections over physical assets only after data on them can no longer be read or recovered. Auditors typically ask for disposal records — per-drive certificates answer that directly.

California · Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.81

California businesses must dispose of customer records containing personal information by erasing or otherwise making them unreadable.

General information, not legal advice. Your compliance obligations depend on your organization — check with your counsel or compliance lead.
