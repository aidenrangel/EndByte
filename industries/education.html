Data destruction for education

Lab computers, staff laptops, and student-information servers hold records that schools are obligated to protect. When they're retired, the data should be retired with them.

Student records outlive the lab computers

Schools and universities retire hardware in big waves — lab refreshes, staff laptop replacements, server migrations. The drives inside hold grades, disciplinary files, health and counseling notes, financial aid records, and staff HR data.

EndByte makes those refreshes simple to document: drives logged by serial at pickup, sanitized to NIST 800-88 or physically destroyed, verified, and certified. Pickups can be scheduled around the academic calendar.

FERPA

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act protects the privacy of student education records. Schools and districts are responsible for keeping those records from being disclosed — including on hardware they retire. Documented, verified destruction closes that gap.

General information, not legal advice. Your compliance obligations depend on your organization — check with your counsel or compliance lead.
