Data destruction for law firms

Confidentiality doesn't end at the recycling bin

Law firms accumulate some of the most sensitive data there is — privileged communications, litigation files, e-discovery productions, client financial and medical records — spread across laptops, document-management servers, backup drives, and multifunction copiers.

EndByte gives firms a disposal process that's easy to defend: every drive logged by serial, sanitized to NIST 800-88 or physically destroyed, verified, and certified. If a client or insurer asks what happened to a retired device, the answer is a certificate, not a shrug.

Duty of confidentiality

ABA Model Rule 1.6(c) asks lawyers to make reasonable efforts to prevent unauthorized disclosure of client information, and California Business & Professions Code § 6068(e) requires attorneys to maintain client confidences inviolate. Secure, documented media disposal is part of meeting that duty.

General information, not legal advice. Your compliance obligations depend on your organization — check with your counsel or compliance lead.
