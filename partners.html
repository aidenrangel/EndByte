Hardware refreshes end with drives nobody wants to handle

Every refresh, office move and offboarding you run for a client leaves a pile of drives behind. Your client needs proof the data is gone — for their auditor, their insurer, or just their peace of mind — and you need it handled without pulling a technician off billable work.

EndByte becomes that last step. Book a pickup for any client site (or drop drives at our bench), and we take it from there: serial-level intake, NIST 800-88 wiping or the standard the client's policy requires, full verification, and physical destruction for anything that fails.

Each job gets its own tracking code you can forward to your client, and the certificate is issued in the client's name — ready to drop into their compliance folder or attach to your closing ticket.
