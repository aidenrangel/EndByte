Job status

Track your job

Enter the job code we gave you at pickup or drop-off (it looks like EB-7K3Q-92XD) to see where your drives are right now.

1
Received

Your drives are in our custody and logged by serial number.

2
Wiping & verifying

Each drive is sanitized to NIST 800-88 (or your required standard), then read back to confirm the wipe. You'll see how many drives are done as they finish. Any drive that fails is set aside for physical destruction.

3
Certificate ready

Your certificate is issued — and you can verify it online with one click.
