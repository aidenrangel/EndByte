If your policy calls for it, yes — and your certificate will say so. But our default is NIST 800-88, the current federal guideline, which replaced the older DoD 5220.22-M approach. For modern hard drives, NIST 800-88 treats a single overwrite pass followed by full verification as sufficient, and extra passes don't make the data any more gone. What matters is the verification, and every drive gets that either way.
