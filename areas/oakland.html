Serving Oakland businesses & residents

Oakland's mix of healthcare systems, logistics and port operations, public agencies, nonprofits, and a growing tech and creative scene means a steady stream of retiring hardware — much of it holding exactly the kind of data regulators care about: patient records, personnel files, financials, and case documents.

EndByte serves Oakland on a scheduled basis from our Santa Clara bench. We confirm a pickup window with you, inventory every drive by serial at your site, and transport it sealed, so the chain of custody is documented from your door to destruction.

Every Oakland job gets the same NIST 800-88 process and per-drive certificates as our South Bay work — reconciled against your intake list so your auditor can match every drive to its final disposition.

What we handle in Oakland

Every job — wherever it is in Oakland — runs the same way: drives are inventoried by serial, sanitized to NIST 800-88 or physically destroyed, verified, and documented with a certificate of destruction. Choose the path that fits:

  • Secure drive wiping — NIST 800-88 sanitization with full-surface verification.
  • Physical destruction — shredding and crushing for failed media and policy-mandated destruction.
  • Pickup & chain of custody — secure collection with serials logged before anything moves.
  • Certificates & audit reporting — a per-drive certificate for your compliance records.
  • Free certified destruction — no-cost for qualifying bulk jobs (100+ drives).
  • Personal & home — single drives and small batches welcome, mail-in available.
