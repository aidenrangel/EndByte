Serving Milpitas businesses & residents

Milpitas is one of Silicon Valley's hardware hubs, home to semiconductor, networking, and electronics companies along with the test labs and data rooms that support them. Retired test systems, engineering workstations, and storage arrays carry design files and customer data that need verifiable destruction.

EndByte serves Milpitas from Santa Clara, about eight miles away. We pick up on your schedule, log every drive by serial before it moves, and reconcile your certificate against your asset list so nothing goes unaccounted for.

Large refreshes of working enterprise drives often qualify for our free certified destruction program — the recovered hardware covers the cost.

What we handle in Milpitas

Every job runs the same way: drives are inventoried by serial, sanitized to NIST 800-88 (or the standard your policy requires) or physically destroyed, verified, and documented with a certificate you can check online.

  • Secure drive wiping — NIST 800-88, DoD 5220.22-M and 20+ other standards, every drive verified.
  • Physical destruction — shredding and crushing for failed media and policy-mandated destruction.
  • Pickup & chain of custody — secure collection with serials logged before anything moves.
  • Certificates & audit reporting — a certificate you can verify online, with a QR code.
  • Free certified destruction — no-cost for qualifying bulk jobs (100+ drives).
  • Personal & home — single drives and small batches welcome, mail-in available.
